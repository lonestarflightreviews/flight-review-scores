# Notes for FRITS #76/TR201

## Made with prior communication.
Before boarding, creating videos onboard, and reviewing this flight, I communicated with and/or asked for permission from the airline. 
This does NOT impede the truth values of the scores and reviews written on this site, as well as in my videos and Instagram posts. 
As usual, I will never lie, cheat, steal, or tolerate those who do.

DISCLAIMER: There was a QR code for the in-flight Wi-Fi-based entertainment called "ScootHub", but unfortunately, the Wi-Fi did not work whatsoever, even after numerous attempts to connect. It would try to connect, then it would declare that the internet was not in range. This indicates that the main Wi-Fi router was either off or not configured, and I decided to omit this from my judgment due to it not being a big deal (it was a 2-hour flight, anyway)
