# Notes for FRITS #74/AK347

## Made with prior communication.
Before boarding, creating videos onboard, and reviewing this flight, I communicated with and/or asked for permission from the airline. 
This does NOT impede the truth values of the scores and reviews written on this site, as well as in my videos and Instagram posts. 
As usual, I will never lie, cheat, steal, or tolerate those who do.

DISCLAIMER: There was Wi-Fi onboard, but I didn't see AirAsia putting any information on the captive portal that users should type 
in order to access the Wi-Fi. The Wi-Fi is programmed in a similar way as ANA, where the system would show that the internet is not 
available to restrict outside access, with users having to enter the captive portal on their own instead of the captive portal being 
auto-deployed like most hotel Wi-Fis. I couldn't find any information about the captive portal or any guide about the Wi-Fi whatsoever, 
other than searching for it before the flight, so I didn't put this aspect into my judgment.
